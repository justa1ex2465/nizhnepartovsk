What is Nizhnepartovsk Duchy?

Nizhnepartovsk Duchy (later referred to as Nizhnepartovsk) is a fictional project like country without any officially recognized land. Nizhnepartovsk was founded on 7th of October, 2025. Nizhnepartovsk is a constitutional monarchy with JustA1ex as head of the state. Official languages are Russian and English.
