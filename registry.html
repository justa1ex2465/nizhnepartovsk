State Office Specifications

👑 The Reigning Duke

How Earned: Supreme hereditary baseline; founder of the Sovereign Throne.

Rights & Privileges: Absolute executive and legislative mandate; holds immediate un-overrideable veto over all state bodies; total legal immunity from all earthly jurisdiction.

Duties & Responsibilities: Preservation of state integrity, maintenance of eternal peace.

📜 The Chancellor

How Earned: Direct executive assignment and appointment by the Duke.

Rights & Privileges: Directs and acts as prime administrator over the entire State Council; holds authority to propose supreme laws and execute citizenship application actions.

Duties & Responsibilities: Reporting status metrics directly to the Crown, scheduling state sessions, and ensuring operational enforcement of the 16 constitutional articles.

💼 State Councilors

How Earned: Designated by the Duke or explicitly selected by the Chancellor.

Rights & Privileges: Head specific ministries (Media, Treasury, or Interior); hold authoritative control over state projects and direct supervision over the actions of elected Deputies.

Duties & Responsibilities: Reviewing raw citizen petitions under the Chancellor's control, maintaining database arrays, and managing specialized spaces like the social media of Nizhnepartovsk.

🏛️ Deputies of the Realm

How Earned: Elected directly through public franchise by the active citizen body.

Rights & Privileges: Voice public opinions and draft basic legislative proposals for consideration by the high chamber.

Duties & Responsibilities: Acting as community liaisons, performing data filtration tasks for applications, and operating under the strict control of the assigned Councilors.
